## 3. Intro

**A**
> We asked creators to tell us their biggest ideas for a chance to win $25,000 in funding.
>
> Hundreds of incredible creators submitted. We were so inspired to see everyone going after something they cared about.
>
> **One idea stood out to us.**

**B**
> \* We asked creators to tell us their biggest ideas for a chance to win $25,000 in funding.
>
> Hundreds of incredible creators [submitted](/submissions), every one of them chasing an idea they couldn't stop thinking about.
>
> **One idea stood out.**

**Why**
- "We were so inspired…" is the first of three *so/incredibly + feeling* lines on the page
  (here, "saw the incredible impact", "We are incredibly inspired and proud"). Three is too
  many — this is the one to cut, because the next two are closer to the people they're about.
- "chasing an idea they couldn't stop thinking about" is a deliberate callback to the old
  homepage's "You've got an idea you can't stop thinking about." Free continuity for anyone
  who saw the original.
- `submitted` is the link to `/submissions`, as you asked. Keeping "incredible" right next to
  it keeps the anchor reading like a sentence and not like a button.
- Dropped "to us" — "One idea stood out." hits harder as the last line before the video.
  (Keep "to us" if you want it humbler; it's a real toss-up.)

## 5. Jackson & David's story

**A**
> Jackson and his dad David Hughens run Stories Made With Love, a channel dedicated to changing how people see the intellectual and developmental disability community.
>
> Their pitch was to build on one of their most impactful stories: the Pinkertons, who adopted 5 boys with Down syndrome.
>
> Since then, they've wanted to go back and make a 5 part docuseries about each member of the family, they just didn't have the resources to do so.
>
> We heard their story and saw the incredible impact they were having. Along with Adobe, we decided this channel was the perfect winner for the big idea fund.

**B**
> Jackson and his dad David Hughens run [Stories Made With Love](#), a channel dedicated to changing how people see the intellectual and developmental disability community.
>
> Their pitch was to build on one of their [most impactful stories](#): the Pinkertons, a family who adopted five boys with Down syndrome.
>
> Ever since, they've wanted to go back and make a five-part docuseries — one episode for each of the boys. They just never had the resources.
>
> We heard their pitch and saw the impact they're already having. Along with Adobe, we knew.

**Why**
- **The reveal was being spent twice.** The old fourth paragraph ends "…we decided this channel
  was the perfect winner for the big idea fund" — and then the very next line is
  "The winner of the first ever Big Idea Fund:". By the time you reach the drumroll, the
  announcement has already happened. Ending on "we knew." holds it for the check photo.
- "Since then" had nothing to point back to — the Pinkertons story is the only prior event, so
  "Ever since" makes that explicit.
- Comma splice fixed ("…about each member of the family, they just didn't have…").
- "5 boys" / "5 part" → "five" / "five-part". Numerals under ten read as copy-paste from a form.
- "each member of the family" → "each of the boys", which is what a five-part series about five
  adopted boys actually is. ⚠️ Confirm — if the series covers the parents too, revert.
- "were having" → "they're already having". Past progressive makes it sound finished.
- "the big idea fund" lowercase → the phrase is capitalised everywhere else on the page.
- "this channel was the perfect winner" — "channel" is cold for a story this personal.

**Both link anchors are preserved:** `Stories Made With Love` and `most impactful stories`.
I need the two URLs (see Open questions).

## 7. After the check

**A**
> We flew out Jackson and David and presented them with the $25,000 check in front of a community of creators and got to sit down with them on Creator Support.
>
> Congratulations Jackson & David. We are so excited to be a part of your journey and see this series.

**B**
> We flew Jackson and David out, handed them the $25,000 check in front of a room full of creators, and sat down with them on Creator Support.
>
> Congratulations, Jackson and David. We can't wait to watch this one get made.

**Why**
- The first sentence was four clauses joined by three "and"s. Now it's a clean list of three.
- "presented them with" → "handed them". "a community of creators" → "a room full of creators",
  which is what the photo shows.
- Missing comma in "Congratulations Jackson & David."
- "be a part of your journey" is the only line on the page that sounds like a brand. "We can't
  wait to watch this one get made" says the same thing and sounds like a person.
- `&` → `and` here; the ampersand is doing real work in "Colin & Samir" and loses force if it's
  everywhere.

## 8. Thank-you box

**A**
> Thank you to everyone who submitted. We are incredibly inspired and proud to be a part of this community.
>
> So much so that we don't want it to end:
>
> `The Big Idea Fund` `Applications Re-Opening Soon`

**B**
> Thank you to everyone who submitted. We're proud to be part of a community this ambitious.
>
> So we're not letting it end here:
>
> `The Big Idea Fund` `Applications Reopening Soon`

**Why**
- "So much so that we don't want it to end" — "it" has no clear referent (the community? the
  fund? the submissions?). "We're not letting it end here" points at the fund and promises
  action rather than a feeling.
- Third "inspired/incredibly" on the page — cut, per §3.
- "Re-Opening" → "Reopening". Standard, and it's set in a yellow highlight where the hyphen
  reads as a line break.

Keep the two yellow highlight bars exactly as designed.
